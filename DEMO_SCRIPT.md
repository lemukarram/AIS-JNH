# Sales demo script — 5 minutes

Before the meeting: log in as **admin**, open the presenter drawer (`P`), pick the client's industry, press **Reset data**. Set Branding (name, colour, logo). Use `F11` for a clean screen.

## Café / Restaurant
| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Overview** | "This is your venue right now: people inside, footfall against yesterday, queue and wait. The numbers update live." Point at the hero count and the occupancy bar. |
| 0:45 | **Live wall** → press `1`…`5` | "Five cameras, every person detected and tracked. Counters agree with the dashboard. No faces, no identity — counts and behaviour only." Toggle **Privacy blur**, **Heatmap**. |
| 1:30 | Presenter `P` → **Queue above threshold** | "A queue builds at the counter — see the lock-on and the alert arrive." Click **Acknowledge** in the feed. |
| 2:15 | **Alerts** | "Every event has a snapshot, rule, confidence and a workflow: new → acknowledged → resolved." Click **Resolve**. |
| 2:45 | **Analytics** | "Peak hours, 90-day trend, conversion funnel and a staffing suggestion for the busy window." |
| 3:30 | **Cameras & zones** | Draw a queue zone on the counter camera → go back to **Live wall**: "Zones you draw apply immediately." |
| 4:15 | **Reports** → *Queue & Service Level* → **PDF** | "Scheduled to your inbox every Sunday." |
| 4:45 | **Request a pilot** | "We connect to your existing CCTV over RTSP/ONVIF — a 1–2 week pilot on a few cameras." |

## Hospital
Switch preset to **Hospital** first (Presenter → Industry).
| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Overview** | "Waiting-room load, restricted-zone events and occupancy in one view." |
| 0:45 | **Live wall** | Lobby, waiting room, corridor, ER entrance, parking. |
| 1:30 | Presenter → **Person down** | "Fall detection: the alert reaches the duty team in seconds." Acknowledge → **Escalate**. |
| 2:15 | Presenter → **Restricted-area entry** | "Radiology corridor is a restricted zone — any entry is flagged." |
| 3:00 | Presenter → **Hand-hygiene missed**, **Stretcher in corridor** | "Compliance and flow, not surveillance of individuals." |
| 3:45 | **Reports** → *Safety Incidents* | "Audit-ready export." |
| 4:30 | **About this demo** | Be upfront: footage and detections here are simulated; a pilot uses your cameras. |

## Handling questions
- **"Is this live?"** No — simulated data in a real interface. The *About this demo* page says exactly what is simulated.
- **"Faces?"** No facial recognition or identity features, by design.
- **Keyboard**: `Ctrl/⌘+K` command palette, `1–5` cameras, `G` layout, `F` fullscreen, `A` alerts, `P` presenter. Language and theme icons are top-right (full Arabic/RTL).
